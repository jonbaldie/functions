The browser driver and cleanup wrapper are retained as
2026-11-06-browser-driver.cjs and 2026-11-06-browser-run.sh. Copy them to
.scratch/browser-journey.js and .scratch/run-browser.sh respectively after
installing the driver in .scratch/driver and the browser in .scratch/browsers.
The wrapper expects the extracted libraries in .scratch/libs/usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu.
The driver uses port 39874 and refuses to start through the wrapper if it is occupied.
The wrapper owns the process group with an exit trap. The driver closes its browser
and terminates and waits for its PHP server, including on SIGINT and SIGTERM.
